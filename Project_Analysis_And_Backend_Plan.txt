1. KYA-KYA HO CHUKA HAI? (Current Status)
------------------------------------------------------
- AI: Google Gemini pipeline (gemini-flash-latest + flash-lite backup).
  Poori PDF ek hi call me analyse hoti hai (~10-30 sec), Vercel ki 60s limit ke andar.
- Scanned PDF (bina text wali) bhi chalti hai: Gemini PDF file ko seedha padhta hai.

  Koi bhi dusre user ka data nahi dekh sakta.
- Upload limits: sirf PDF, max 4 MB, rate limit.
- Frontend: Upload screen ka crash fix, graph readable aur fast, history me delete,
  details page me AI ka roadmap, mobile layout, saaf error messages.
- Tests: server ke 9 automated tests (`npm test`) + Playwright se poora flow check kiya.
